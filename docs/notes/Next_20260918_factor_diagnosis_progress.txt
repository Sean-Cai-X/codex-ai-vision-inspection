Interpretation
On these fixtures, scale variation is the largest single tested factor;
intensity reduction is also material, especially q2. Translation contributes
much less in this small displacement range. Centered angle-only results are
below the existing numerical MAE1/P952 reference limits, but this diagnostic
does not confer independent validation or arbitrary-angle production approval.
The combined case reproduces all previous v2 holdout angle predictions within
1e-5 degree, supporting that these controls use the intended image/model path.
Effects are not additive: do not sum individual error increases to explain the
combined error. Signed angle shifts can reinforce or cancel one another.
Scale tests rerender geometry AND its GT mask; brightness-only tests preserve
the mask. This does not identify a specific faulty layer or prove BN is the
cause; it measures end-to-end frozen-model sensitivity.
Only one checkpoint, three oriented synthetic shapes, small images, prescribed
factor values, GT masks and known q were tested. No unseen shape, real image,
occlusion, predicted mask, CUDA or Windows assertion is supported.
