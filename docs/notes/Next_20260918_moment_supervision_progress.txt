Change and mathematical contract
The previous softmax(kappa*cos(q*(bin-theta))) target has higher harmonics.
On a finite K grid, those can alias into the q-th moment used for decoding.
The experimental training target is now:
  p[g] = (1 + 2*rho*cos(q*(2*pi*g/K-theta))) / K, rho=.4.
For q>0 and K>2q, uniform-grid root-of-unity sums yield exactly:
  sum(p[g])=1
  sum(p[g]*exp(i*q*2*pi*g/K))=rho*exp(i*q*theta).
Probabilities stay strictly positive for 0<rho<.5. q0 uses a uniform target
and remains excluded from angle supervision. Concentration is not confidence.
Only training supervision changed; the network, decoder and optimizer did not.

Interpretation and boundaries
The former q4 target bias is fixed, and small-sample overfit now passes.
The new holdout still fails, primarily q1/q2 in this combined shift.
Its angles differ from v1, so aggregate v1/v2 holdout numbers are NOT a
same-input model comparison. No claim that generic accuracy improved.
Ideal-target error alone is not learned-model accuracy. Likewise known q0
gating is not learned shape/symmetry recognition.
This experiment uses GT masks, one instance/image, four analytic shapes,
one training seed and tiny images. Real masks, occlusion, unknown shapes,
multi-instance operation, real images and CUDA are not validated here.
The current sweep mixes angle, scale, intensity and placement. It cannot
identify which nuisance factor caused each residual model error.
