Implemented torch_cyclic_analytic_group.h/.cpp:
- Explicit [B,C,K,H,W] input/output.
- Reuses AnalyticLift's basis generator as a registered submodule.
- Coefficients [Cout,Cin*K,6] represent relative input directions.
- Each output direction g uses analytically steered spatial kernels and
  relative orientation h-g (positive roll of the input-kernel axis).
- Stride 1, same zero padding, no cache or independent per-output kernels.
- Constructor/input checks and inherited archive contract validation.

No production runtime, V1 model, FastMatch, image asset or Evidence catalog
is changed. Existing concurrent FastMatch/open-boundary work is excluded.

tests/cyclic_pose/stack_test.cpp exercises:
  AnalyticLift -> SiLU -> AnalyticGroup -> SiLU -> AnalyticGroup -> SiLU
against a matched pixel-interpolated stack. Every unrotated pixel kernel
is copied from the corresponding analytic bank before testing.
Two coefficient seeds, K=8/12/24, steps 1, K/4, K/4+1, K-1.
The 18 non-quarter-turn combinations share one fixed smooth image family;
they are not independent business images or trained-model evaluations.
Errors are relative RMS in the central 13x13 of a 49x49 field (crop 18).
This excludes most boundary contamination; it does not establish full-frame
padding robustness.

Predeclared regression gates:
- Every controlled smooth interior layer: error < 0.10.
- Quarter-turn stacked/random group-only error < 5e-5.
- Final-layer analytic mean below matched interpolated mean.
- Nonzero reference energy, finite nonzero gradients in all three banks.
- Wrong orientation-only action (without spatial rotation) must be detected.
- Exact group archive roundtrip and rejection of wrong K/dtype/shape/options.
