Implementation
--------------
Private SegCandidate retains source level, grid row/column and global anchor ID
(level-major, then row-major; identity assigned before confidence filtering).
Optional SegCandidateAudit retains ranked candidates and exact NMS disposition:
selected, suppressed_box_nms (with suppressor anchor and box IoU), or
unexamined_detection_cap. A cap-skipped candidate is NOT labelled suppressed.
Sorting, class-max decode, thresholds and selection logic are unchanged.
No new public DLL exports or typed ABI fields. The audit is an opt-in internal
hook, exercised by the native R&D executable, NOT a shipped business UI feature.

The R&D runner records seven probabilities, winner/target margin, network and
original-image boxes, original-image mask IoU and valid-contour status for up
to 128 probes per image. Selected candidates are always probed first; remaining
probes are ranked by box IoU against the raster-GT envelope, irrespective of
class/NMS decision. This oracle selection is ONLY diagnostic, never inference.
All above-threshold candidates have rank/identity/disposition records. Below-
threshold masks are NOT probed. The report explicitly says that failure to find
a qualified target within the probes does NOT prove no such candidate exists.
Duplicate-pair accounting excludes invalid contours, matching the final output.

Machine-readable explanations include:
QUALIFIED_TARGET_SUPPRESSED_BY_WRONG_CLASS
QUALIFIED_TARGET_SUPPRESSED_BY_SAME_CLASS
QUALIFIED_TARGET_UNEXAMINED_AT_CAP
SELECTED_GEOMETRY_MATCH_WRONG_CLASS
These are positive evidence of observed mechanisms, not exclusive root causes
or model-accuracy approval. Qualified means mask IoU >= .5 and valid contour.

above-threshold anchor is a false positive (many cover the same target).

Correction direction, not an implemented quality fix
----------------------------------------------------
1. Keep eval inference, seven-class semantics and fixed thresholds. Audit/train
   class competition and foreground score calibration at geometrically good
   anchors; ranking quality must be measured before and after NMS.
2. Track target recall at the bounded candidate stage separately from score
   rank and final count. Increasing max_detections alone can expose candidates
   without fixing false positives; it must not count as a model improvement.
3. Evaluate box/mask consistency and duplicate control separately. Switching
   to class-aware NMS alone may retain correct AND wrong class duplicates.
4. Retain the same failed image groups for the next controlled training arm;
   require per-image class/count/geometry regressions, not only best IoU.
