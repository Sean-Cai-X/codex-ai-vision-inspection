What changed
------------
CxInstanceMaskErrorAnalysis implements a SINGLE-GT class-agnostic matching
diagnostic. It separates target-class best IoU from any-class best IoU and
reports selected class, unmatched GT/predictions, pair overlap, mask-bbox IoU,
centroid distance, area ratio and symmetric mean boundary distance.
Scores do not determine the geometric match. Highest IoU wins; stable ID breaks
ties. Thresholds are explicit (matching .5, boundary diagnostic .9), not a
production accuracy gate. ClassConfusion requires a geometrically matched wrong
class, not merely the presence of another-class candidate. LocalizationMiss
means no returned candidate reaches the matching threshold; it does not prove
the detector never generated a correct candidate before NMS.
Unlabelled VERIFY keeps GT-dependent metrics null. An empty labelled GT is an
error, not unlabelled data. Wrong mask size/type, invalid class/score, duplicate
IDs and invalid thresholds are rejected. No image is rescaled to hide mismatch.
This interface does NOT yet implement general multiple-GT matching.
