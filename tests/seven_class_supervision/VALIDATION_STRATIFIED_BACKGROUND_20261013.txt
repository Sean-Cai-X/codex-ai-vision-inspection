Fixed-total stratified hard-background comparison, 2026-10-13

Decision: reject this allocation as a business-model improvement. Some best
mask IoUs improve, but target counts, wrong classes and duplicate pairs worsen.
Effective target matching at IoU >= .5 is unchanged. No publication, default
change, production approval or candidate activation. Native local C++/LibTorch
only; no Python, gateway, remote host or business image access.

Single controlled change: unified_v14_stratified_background_experimental
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Keep v13 foreground CE, ordinary background sigmoid BCE, single-owner positive
assignment, box/DFL/mask losses and hard-loss weight 2. Replace global hard-
background Top-K with per-level quotas: floor(K/5), floor(K/5), remainder.
For the actual ten positives per exposure, K=200 and P3/P4/P5 quotas=40/40/120.
If a level lacks negatives, globally fill remaining slots from unselected
negatives. Positive anchors are excluded; no duplicates; total K preserved.
Hard loss remains a single mean over the selected K anchors, not an unweighted
mean of three per-level losses. Quotas change sampling, not the decoder.
The new option defaults OFF and is R&D test code only.
