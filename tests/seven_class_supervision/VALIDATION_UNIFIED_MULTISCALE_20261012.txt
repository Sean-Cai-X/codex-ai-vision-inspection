Finding: on all 5 circle and all 5 ellipse samples, the global top-10
assignment chooses 0/0/10 anchors in the 80x80/40x40/20x20 levels. On the
synthetic VERIFY images the best eligible decoded-box IoU is:
                     80x80    40x40    20x20
  circle              0.073    0.270    0.963
  ellipse             0.121    0.456    0.975
The top-10 means are respectively 0.071/0.263/0.955 for circle and
0.113/0.426/0.966 for ellipse. This directly explains why treating 10
fine-scale anchors as equally reliable box/mask targets is hazardous.

The production decoder takes the maximum of seven sigmoid class scores for
each anchor. On synthetic VERIFY, 8,291/8,400 circle anchors and 8,292/8,400
ellipse anchors clear confidence 0.25. All 1,600 anchors of the 40x40 level
and all 400 of the 20x20 level clear it; the rest are mostly 80x80 anchors.
The 80x80 target-class mean score inside/outside the GT bbox is 0.441/0.442
for circle and 0.336/0.302 for ellipse. These statistics establish weak
spatial discrimination under this parent and budget. They do not prove a
single root cause for the business images.

Follow-up v9 (same local date, same fixed budget)

  head has bins 0..15. At stride 8, a 640-pixel input can represent less than
  240 pixels of total width from one 80x80 anchor. These synthetic circle and
  ellipse boxes are about 360 input pixels wide after 64-to-640 letterbox.
  This geometric limit is consistent with the measured 80x80 IoU below 0.21.
