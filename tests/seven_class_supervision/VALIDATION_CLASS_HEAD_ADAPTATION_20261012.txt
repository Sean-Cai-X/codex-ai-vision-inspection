Class-head differential LR experiment, local R&D, 2026-10-12

Question: can the seven-class cv3 head adapt faster than the geometry branches
without breaking the mask/box quality obtained by unified v5 assignment?

The native C++ runner strict-loads the registered development parent and
trains three arms per shape from the same initialization: frozen legacy v1
reference, unified v5, and unified v10. All arms use the same generated
3 Train/1 Valid/1 held-out synthetic VERIFY split, seed 20261012, 40 epochs,
40 optimizer updates, batch size 2, cosine schedule, train-mode BN during
training, and eval-mode validation/inference. The production score, NMS,
max20, mask and contour decode remain unchanged. Parent/source inventories
and parent manifest/checkpoint/attestation/lineage hashes are checked.

V10 changes only the optimizer parameter grouping. Twenty-four cv3 class-head
parameter tensors use 5x LR; 195 other tensors use baseline LR. The recorded
effective LR goes from 0.0005 to 0.000005 for cv3 and 0.0001 to 0.000001 for
the others. The v5 loss, global top-10 positive assignment, class/box/DFL/mask
owner and hard-negative formula are unchanged.

The v10 circle target count improves, but wrong-class results worsen. V10
ellipse has 16 target candidates across 5 one-object images and 73 wrong-class
candidates. Its high best-mask IoU cannot compensate for those count/class
errors. All v10 images still hit the max20 output cap; full post-NMS survivor
count remains unknown when cap examination stops. Reducing pre-NMS score count
alone is not a quality pass.

Decision: reject v10 for candidate promotion. Keep v5 as the geometry-only
baseline. The next training-data study must use explicit seven-class positive
and hard-negative examples rather than further learning-rate tuning on a
three-image single-class synthetic split. It needs a frozen revision and an
independent VERIFY group before it can support a generalization claim.

No business runtime, ABI, default profile, registered parent, frozen business
revision or business image changed. Development trial only; tickets remain
