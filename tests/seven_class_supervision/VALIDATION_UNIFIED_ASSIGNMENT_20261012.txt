Unified assignment, local R&D experiment, 2026-10-12

Scope
- Registered development parent: dev_parent_yolov8seg_7class_warmup_20260915.
- Binding: contracts/development_parent_test001_20261009.json. The runner verifies
  the parent manifest, checkpoint, attestation and lineage SHA256 before training,
  and verifies the parent/source asset inventories after training.
- Native C++/LibTorch only. No Python training, business image, or business
  frozen dataset was used. The parent is loaded strictly, 417/417 keys.
- Fixed order: arc, circle, ellipse, line, open_curve, polygon, closed_curve.
- Synthetic circle and ellipse each have 3 Train, 1 Valid, 1 held-out VERIFY.
  VERIFY has a synthetic oracle annotation only during offline evaluation; the
  model does not see that annotation during training or model selection.
- Each arm: seed 20261012, 40 epochs, 40 optimizer updates, batch size 2,
  Adam, cosine effective LR 1e-4 to 1e-6, BN train statistics in training,
  eval mode for validation and inference. The production score/NMS/max20 and
  mask decoder are used unchanged during evaluation.

Algorithm under test
- Anchor assignment uses real grid centers inside GT, top-10 score*IoU^6
  per GT, and one deterministic GT owner per anchor.
- The same owner index drives class, box IoU, DFL and mask targets. Unassigned
  anchors provide background classification supervision.
- The retained v5 experiment balances positive and negative class logits and
  adds 2x loss for the 20 highest-loss negative logits per positive anchor.
- This is a test-only loss implementation. It does not change the runtime DLL,
  C ABI, business training defaults, current parent or deployed candidate.

Target/wrong are counts of accepted post-mask candidates summed over images;
zero is images with no target-class candidate; bestIoU is the mean of each
image's best target-class mask IoU against the synthetic oracle. An image has
one true object, so multiple target candidates represent count errors. V5 has
zero target-target mask pairs above IoU 0.5; this does not prove all other
duplicate or fragment errors are gone. All arms still have roughly 8,200-8,400
class-score candidates before NMS and reach the max20 output cap. The recorded
full-post-NMS count is unknown when the cap stops examination.

Decision
- V5 improves synthetic target localization and target count, but its wrong
  class counts (62/shape) exceed legacy v1 (16 circle, 30 ellipse). It fails
  joint count/class/mask acceptance and is NOT an upgrade candidate for the
  typed business bridge. V6 regresses further and is not retained in code.
- The business tickets RND-VAI-BUSINESS-TRIAL-20261012 and
  RND-VAI-TESTCIRCLE-20261012 remain NOT_PASSED. This synthetic experiment is
  not a result on their frozen business revisions and is not independent
  business validation.
- Next experiment should diagnose per-class raw logit distributions and
  task-aligned positive/negative ownership against wrong-class NMS survivors,
  then compare under the same fixed budget. Do not use max20, score threshold,
  or class filtering as a substitute for correcting the raw classification.
